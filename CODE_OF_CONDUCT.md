# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Awesome Tools community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy towards other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer at [wangruofeng007@gmail.com](mailto:wangruofeng007@gmail.com). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated, and an appropriate response will follow.
